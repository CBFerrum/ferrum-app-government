# Provenance

This app's design and code are its own. No code was copied from another project.

- Its shape follows common practice for permits and licences (an application, its documents and
  fee, a review, an issued permit with its expiry) and for citizens' cases with service levels.
- The fees are ERP's Sales Invoices, and their payment is ERP's, read through the platform's
  records as Nonprofit reads its memberships'.
- The portal page follows Helpdesk's "Your tickets" and Property's "Your home".
